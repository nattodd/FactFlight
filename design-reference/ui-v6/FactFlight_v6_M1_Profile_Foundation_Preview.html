Parent settings & scoring

Microphone permissions: choose “Allow while visiting the site”, not “Allow this time”. This app keeps the same listening session from “ready” into the quiz. Chrome may still stop recognition. The app makes limited automatic reconnect attempts when the browser allows them. Safari may not reveal permission status; in that case, the app tries once before asking for a manual reconnect. Browser permission prompts cannot be bypassed. A hosted HTTPS page or localhost provides a stable site address for permissions. The app cannot override browser permission rules.

First-try score counts facts answered correctly before any wrong answer or skip. Corrections are tracked separately.

Voice recognition may use your browser’s online speech service. No audio is saved by this app. Results stay in this browser; export them to keep a backup.
