## Report and implementation

The owner reported persistent iPhone microphone-use indicators after completing a voice flight, backgrounding for 30 seconds and locking/unlocking. Navigating Home or menus first reportedly cleared them. The exact browser for this observation was not specified. This remains a physical-device release gate, separate from the accepted Mode H delay.

The previous flow cleared the recognizer reference on natural end and skipped background cleanup after the flight entered Done. The local change retains only the latest naturally ended iOS recognizer for terminal cleanup. Completion, cancellation, menu navigation and backgrounding invalidate pending restarts, detach active callbacks, request abort for retained recognition and cancel iOS synthesis. Background cleanup now applies even on Results and on desktop. An already ended recognizer does not wait for a second end event; an active iOS recognizer still requires confirmed shutdown and the existing cooldown before reuse of the microphone.

Natural phrase endings, fresh recognizers, the rate-2 cue and 400 ms inter-phrase cooldown remain unchanged. The tests verify release requests and no stale restart; they cannot verify that WebKit honors the request at the hardware level. No extra audio stream, recording, remote service or permission request was added.

## Parent corner

Disable microphone persists under `fact-flight-microphone-disabled-v1`, separate from learner results and backups. It covers all learners and Guest in this browser profile/origin. Ordinary and missed-review voice buttons become disabled and read Parent restricted. Remembered Quick/repeat/review launches use keyboard/touch when restricted, and direct reconnect/start paths are guarded. Another open tab receives the storage change and stops voice. The standalone lab reads only this restriction key and blocks/cancels recognition too.

Storage-read failure blocks voice. A failed setting write leaves voice blocked in the current tab and reports that the preference could not be saved; it does not claim lasting enforcement. Re-enabling does not automatically start capture. Existing learner history and its schema are unchanged.

This is a preference, not a PIN lock: anyone able to open Parent corner can change it. It cannot control other browsers/origins, survive cleared website data, or revoke OS permission. The UI explains these limits and points to browser/device permissions for broader enforcement.
