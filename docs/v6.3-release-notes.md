## More intentional practice

Ordinary practice now uses shared mathematical content definitions and activity-specific Level 2/3 allocations to give selected-tier facts a deliberate share while retaining review. Zero/identity limits are ceilings, not quotas. The generator retains original allowed pools, Level 1/4/5 behavior, and bounded fallback rules for tiny pools. Allocation carryover is kept in memory per learner and resets with the page; it is not added to learner backups.

The [educator guide](what-children-practice.md) includes generated graphs, definitions and measured repetition tradeoffs across all eight activities and Levels 1–5. Its audit uses synthetic questions; it does not establish improved learning outcomes.

## Multiplication and division families

Choose multiplication tables or division families 1–12, with explicit ranges through 10 or through 12. Repeats, remembered setup, Next Table(s)/Divisor(s), reports and readiness retain the selected range. Next choices stop at 12 without wrapping or dropping part of a mixed selection.

## Clearer menus and Progress

Operations & Facts and Quick Practice show all eight activities directly. Future subject and Math choices remain disabled tiles with Coming Soon badges. Icons and borders adapt to the theme, and badges align at the bottom.

Progress includes Sessions, First-try facts and Practice time, followed by initially collapsed Mastery and Results. Expanded activities use the full report width. Parents can inspect readiness requirements, what to practice next, comparable results, fact patterns and flight logs. Results distinguishes encounters from generated questions and incorrect submissions from unresolved questions. Table-content totals can overlap; overall encounters count once. Higher factors seen in numbered levels are separate from selectable families.

## Readiness and optional parent focus

Readiness uses bounded recent evidence, aggregate first-try accuracy, separate completed flights and distinct content coverage. Short flights contribute; a single error does not automatically erase a run. Numbered levels use the outer 12-flight window with a 60-encounter cap; chosen families use 8 flights and 40 encounters. Completion credits come from the outer window. Timed School achievements remain separate. These rules describe current practice readiness, not proven long-term retention.

Mastered choices show trophies. Parent corner offers Focus on unmastered practice, off by default and separate for each learner. Parents can allow one review flight; missed-fact review remains available. Mixed selections explain restricted choices, Guest does not inherit another learner's preference, and active flights are not interrupted. This is a lightweight preference, not an authenticated parental lock.

## Compatibility and preserved behavior

Existing learner backups and historical records remain readable without rewriting their answers. Backups containing new selected division or extended-table scopes require v6.3; v6.2 is not a reader for all new backups. Preserve an original production backup before testing copies. Focus preferences are separate from learner exports.

The original multiplication School Test retains its 60 questions, two-minute limit, tables 2–10, layout, generation and scoring. Voice remains optional, with keyboard/keypad fallback. The known iOS microphone retry limitation remains; this release does not claim a speech fix. No cloud synchronization, remote learner analytics or audio recording has been added.
