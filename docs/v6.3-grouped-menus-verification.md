## Navigation

Learner Home retains Quick Practice and the Math subject. Following owner preview feedback, future subjects use disabled tiles with explicit Coming Soon badges; Numbers & Counting and Time & Clocks use the same treatment under Math. Math offers Operations & Facts and the original School Test shortcut; Numbers & Counting, Time & Clocks and later Money & Measurement remain unavailable and explicitly labelled.

Operations & Facts shows all eight activity tiles directly, in the existing order:

| First four | Following four |
|---|---|
| Addition | Number bonds |
| Subtraction | Doubles |
| Multiplication | Halves |
| Division | Squares |

The owner's refinement on 2026-11-18 removes the intermediate Add & Subtract / Multiply & Divide / Doubles & Halves category screens. The small registry now contains display order only. There is no extra group click for any activity.

Quick Practice opens the same eight tiles directly, bypassing the Math overview. A remembered Quick Start again still launches directly. Practice Again, Next Level, Next Table(s)/Divisor(s), and historical review keep their existing setup/launch semantics. Table/divisor selection remains inside activity setup.

Back returns to the preceding screen and focuses the tile that opened it. Forward navigation focuses the heading. Home returns to learner Home; active-flight exit confirmation remains in place. Existing bottom action bars and short-screen flow behavior are preserved. Controls retain large targets and text/icon labels.
