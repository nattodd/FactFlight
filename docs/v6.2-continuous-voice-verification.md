## Implemented behavior

- One continuous iOS recognizer spans ready and answers until an explicit stop or unexpected browser end. Results are processed as they arrive, with existing interim/final deduplication and question guards. No per-answer synthesis, natural-end scoring, recurring cooldown or Audio Session category override remains in the quiz.
- Completion, setup cancellation, confirmed navigation away, parent restriction and supported background/pagehide events invalidate pending work, clear timers, detach callbacks and request abort on the active recognizer. Home during an active flight retains the existing confirmation and clock behavior; Finish & review ends the flight.
- Unexpected iOS natural endings and reported errors do not automatically restart. Returning to the foreground also never restarts capture. A new deliberate start creates a fresh instance. Desktop retains its existing continuous recognition and automatic recovery policy.
- Manual replacement of an active iOS recognizer waits for abort-end plus 400 ms. Missing confirmation after 2.5 seconds blocks overlapping capture and offers recovery. This limited shutdown gate is not a per-answer delay or proof of hardware release.
- Missing start confirmation times out after eight seconds. After start, readiness with no results times out after eight seconds; during a flight the no-result interval is twenty seconds. Speechstart alone does not clear the no-result watchdog. The attempt is stopped, never automatically retried. A learner thinking silently can also reach this timeout; the message reports missing results, not a diagnosed browser bug. Answers remain available and the existing timer continues.
- Browser-reported permission/service restrictions, interruption, service errors, missing start confirmation and missing results have distinct guidance. Unsupported recognition retains typed fallback. No new audio-capture mechanism, dependency, permission or external service is introduced.
- Try microphone again remains prominent. Tap or type instead returns to the same setup, preserving ordinary or mixed-review selection without beginning a flight. After a failed retry, setup refresh options expand. Refresh retains the existing one-shot ten-minute tab-local setup, owner validation, Guest/unsaved/pending-save guards and deliberate restart requirement. It cannot guarantee recognition recovery and is unavailable during a running flight.
- Exact iOS aliases remain: sex → 6, thor/poor → 4, freddy → ready in setup only. They are independent of the expected answer. No extra commands are required in normal practice.
- Parent restriction remains browser-origin/profile-local, persistent and cross-tab aware, covering learners and Guest. Re-enabling does not start voice. It is not a PIN lock or OS permission revocation.

Mobile bottom actions, missed-fact review, per-table progress, Show all controls, borders and worksheet layout are preserved. Math generators, profile storage/integration and the menu stylesheet are unchanged. No history schema or stored records are rewritten. Existing timing warnings remain accurate because active time includes listening, input and retries even without deliberate cue delays.
