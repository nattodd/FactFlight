# v6.3 local Progress dashboard verification

## Scope and authorization

Owner-authorized local implementation on `v6.3-development`, 2026-11-18. No commit, push, deployment or publication. The private Learner Stats Review Mock Dashboard was used as a visual reference only. No private HTML, screenshots, learner exports or embedded data were copied into the repository. Review screenshots use the invented records in `tests/progress-fixtures.cjs`.

This increment connects existing pure content, reporting and readiness functions to Progress. It changes no generator, School Test, voice implementation, saved-record schema or importer. Existing learner backup compatibility remains unchanged: older backups are readable; new division-family/through-12 records introduced by the preceding increment require v6.3.

## Implemented behavior

- Three summary cards remain side by side, including at 320 pixels. Mastery and Results start collapsed. Activities follow the eight-activity production order with equal collapsed tile dimensions at each width.
- Mastery uses all available learner evidence, independently of Results filters. Status text accompanies trophies, tools, flags and striped untried indicators. Each level/family has a progress bar, evidence requirements and an actionable next requirement. Later evidence can lower readiness; nothing is written back as a permanent achievement.
- The overall facts bar averages 88 selectable practice scopes equally: 40 numbered levels and 48 multiplication/division family-range choices. Nine multiplication School Test achievements appear separately. Mastered means current facts readiness under `readiness-v1`; it does not establish retention or disable practice.
- Results filters cover activity, flight length, input and completion. Logs sort by date or first-try accuracy. Summary cards follow Results filters; the on-screen note explicitly distinguishes the unchanged Mastery evidence.
- Activity details include levels/chosen setups, question counts, explicit denominators and inferred content buckets. Personal records compare exact setups, including range, count, mode, input, time limit and target; clean and corrected completions have separate times and sample counts.
- Multiplication content reports show selectable tables 1–12, an independent all/latest-five filtered-flight window, and a collapsed higher-factor section. Overlapping attribution is explained; higher factors have no mastery badge or selection action. Facts to focus on retain incorrect submitted values, wrong-submission counts and average recorded active time.
- Local refinement (2026-11-19): opened activity cards span the full grid width in Mastery and Results; closed desktop cards retain two columns. “What to do next” lists all unmet checks as parent-friendly actions, such as finishing two more short flights and practising nine more different facts. Exact evidence counts and rules remain available under a separate, initially collapsed “How readiness is calculated” disclosure. The dashboard suite verifies both expanded widths, restored collapsed width, guidance text and hidden calculation details at 320, 390 and 1440 pixels. Synthetic screenshots were refreshed; no calculation or storage changes.
- Native disclosures support keyboard operation. Bottom Collapse buttons return focus and scroll to their own summary. Learner selection, missed-fact review, individual-flight inspection and backup controls remain available.
- JSON export always includes the whole selected learner, including available unsaved flights; CSV uses filtered whole flights. Mixed reviews remain whole flights in summary cards, logs and CSV, while activity detail counts only matching entries. Guest is adapted only in memory, remains separate and has no export controls. Previous progress retains its archive export and read-error notice.

## Synthetic acceptance examples

The fixture contains 10 flights, 72 generated questions, 71 encountered questions, 60 first-try successes, 11 incorrect submissions and one untouched question. Recorded elapsed practice totals 108 seconds.

- Six five-question Squares Level 3 flights cover both new bases. One corrected error yields 29/30 first try and earns readiness; it does not reset all earlier practice.
- Thirty correct Addition Level 3 encounters repeat one family. The report shows 100% accuracy, but inadequate family coverage prevents a trophy.
- Ten unresolved Subtraction errors produce Needs work. An untouched Division flight contributes to Sessions but supplies no readiness evidence.
- A numbered-level `16 × 7` encounter contributes once overall, once under table 7 and once under higher factor 16.
- Filtering to Squares displays 6 sessions, 29 first-try facts and 45 seconds, but JSON export still contains all 10 unchanged flight records. Another learner and Guest see no records from this fixture.
