## Rollback decision and data protection

The owner explicitly requires separate approval for rollback. Stop and request that approval before any rollback/revert or production recovery deployment; do not treat production promotion approval as rollback authorization. Preserve failure evidence and export valued learner JSON through a functioning v6.1 reader before changing delivery where possible. Do not upload private backups to automated test services.

**If no targeted v6.1 records exist on the affected production origins:** a reviewed normal revert of the release merge to restore the v6.0.0 app may be suitable, after backups and confirmation that no incompatible records were created/imported. Identify the actual promotion commit; use the appropriate normal revert for its merge method, never a hard reset/force-push. Verify the resulting public files and old records. Do not delete release tags or history to conceal the failed release.

**If targeted records exist or their presence is unknown:** do not treat a whole-app v6.0 revert as data-compatible. v6.0 does not validate/import schema-2 targeted backups and can mislabel or mishandle their settings. Prefer a reviewed forward fix or narrowly revert the faulty UI behavior while retaining the v6.1 reader, labels and backup support. This requires implementation and testing at incident time; no such emergency patch is claimed prepared here. If launch must be restricted, obtain approval for a temporary restriction that preserves access to history/exports rather than clearing data.

If an emergency v6.0 restoration is nevertheless explicitly approved, document that targeted sessions may not display/replay correctly and their backups cannot be restored there. Preserve originals and avoid editing/deleting those records; plan recovery through a corrected v6.1-compatible app on the original origin. Never infer deletion solely from records being invisible or mislabeled. No automatic migration/downgrade is part of this release.

After rollback/recovery, verify the exact published version and data readability, record the outcome and update release notes transparently. Netlify production remains untouched throughout unless separately approved.
