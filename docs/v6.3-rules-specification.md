## 2. Practice scopes and shared content definitions

Derive one pure classification from activity definitions for generation, reporting and readiness; do not duplicate prototype text-parsing heuristics.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Selected scope | Numbered level, chosen multiplication tables, chosen division divisors, School Test or review |
| Allowed pool | Valid facts for that scope and range |
| Earliest eligible level | First numbered pool containing the family, or outside numbered pools |
| Identity flags | Activity-specific zero/identity patterns |
| Family identity | Canonical equivalent family for coverage and repeat avoidance |
| Display identity | Exact equation or prompt |

Group reversed addition/multiplication. Preserve subtraction/division order. Bonds retain total and known/missing-part semantics. Selected level is distinct from earliest eligible level; label earlier content as **Earlier-level review**, not cognitive difficulty. Historical classification is inferred from recorded facts; never invent generator provenance.

Before implementation, enumerate identity flags and boundary fixtures for every activity against the actual pools. Do not classify all ×2, ×5 or ×10 questions as freebies. Resolve impossible or empty-tier cases explicitly rather than silently dropping coverage requirements.

## 3. Multiplication through 12 and division families

| Activity | Selectable scope | Range choice |
|---|---|---|
| Multiplication | Tables 1–12 | Other factor up to 10 / up to 12 |
| Division | Divide by 1–12 | Answers up to 10 / up to 12 |
| School Test | Existing tables 2–10 | Fixed original content |

Ranges start at 1. Existing multiplication selections retain their up-to-10 meaning. Choosing divisor 4 through 12 permits 4 ÷ 4 through 48 ÷ 4 with exact answers, not arbitrary dividends. Readiness belongs to each family and range together. Evidence for members 1–10 may contribute to both range scopes, but through-12 readiness requires members 11 and 12 too.

Next Table(s) and Next Divisor(s) advance all selected values by one, stop if any value is 12, and never wrap or drop a choice. Preserve ranges in preferences, repeats, exports and validation. No silent reinterpretation of historical selections.

## 4. Question-selection rules

**Review priority — 2026-11-18:** At the owner's request, audit all activities at Levels 1–5 before further selector/UI work. The [current-pool, fact-type and sequence audit](v6.3-question-mix-audit.md) documents exact mixes, repeats and structural similarities and proposes revised balances for review. Its proposed percentages do not supersede the approved defaults below until accepted. No generator change is included.

Use mutually exclusive buckets: remove identities first, then divide eligible non-identity families into selected-tier and earlier-tier material.

| Scope | Approved starting policy |
|---|---|
| Squares, division and multiplication Level 3 | 65% selected-tier, 25% earlier review, maximum 10% identities |
| Doubles and halves Level 3 | Same starting target, subject to synthetic distribution review |
| Addition and subtraction Level 3 | Balanced non-identity pool sampling; 10% identity ceiling and run control |
| Levels 1–2 | Retain balanced family rounds initially; avoid identity runs where feasible |
| Levels 4–5 | Retain allowed pools; introduce no earlier material |
| Chosen tables/divisors | Balance requested families; no blanket identity cap |
| School Test | Unchanged |

Unused identity capacity goes first to eligible selected-tier non-identity facts, then non-identity review. A ceiling is not a quota. Never introduce identities or earlier facts into a pool that does not contain them. Choosing table/divisor 1 intentionally requests identities.

For allocation-based scopes, use a bounded 20-question cycle: 13 selected-tier slots, 5 earlier-review slots and at most 2 identity slots when feasible. Spread slots through the cycle. Carry position across flights for the same learner/scope in preferences separate from history; Guest carryover is temporary. Advance when a question is encountered, not merely generated. Distinguish generated and encountered distributions. Four consecutive five-question flights satisfy a feasible cycle; individual short flights need not match exact percentages.

Fallback priority: valid activity/range and correct answer; feasible allocation/identity ceiling; avoid adjacent identical displayed equations; avoid recent families and more than two consecutive identities; prefer varied answers/structures. Use bounded selection and relax diversity only as needed, never range validity. Preserve balanced rounds within buckets. Squares Level 3 has only two new bases (11 and 12); deliberately explain and test its increased repetition.

Before implementation, specify reservation/encounter bookkeeping for pre-generated worksheets, skips, revisits, Undo, stopped flights, reload and failed preference writes. A fact is not a new encounter merely because it is revisited. Test the accepted encounter-driven cycle rather than silently replacing it with generated-question accounting. Version the generator policy for future flights only, after the backup/compatibility gate.

## 5. Readiness evidence: approved default

Keep the **Mastery** section and explain: “Ready under this practice rule. This does not measure long-term retention.” Current readiness controls optional focus restrictions.

| Requirement | Numbered level | One chosen table/divisor |
|---|---|---|
| Recent window | Latest 60 encounters within latest 12 contributing flights | Latest 40 encounters within latest 8 contributing flights |
| Minimum encounters | 30 | 20 |
| Minimum completed contributing flights | 3 | 2 |
| Aggregate first-try accuracy | At least 95% | At least 95% |
| Tier exposure | At least 10 selected-tier non-identity encounters | Every factor/answer in selected range encountered |
| Distinct coverage | All families when the tier has one or two; otherwise min(10, ceiling(30% of selected-tier non-identity families)) | All 10 or all 12 members |

A contributing flight contains an attributable encounter. Apply the flight limit first, then retain the newest encounters up to the cap. **Owner-approved correction during foundation implementation:** count completed contributing flights across the outer 12/8-flight window, even if their encounters fall outside the 60/40 cap. Accuracy and coverage still use only retained encounters. Return both completion-credit and capped-encounter flight IDs; this preserves a viable route for repeated long flights. Order flights by saved date (session ID breaks ties), and encounters by first-submission offset (item index is a deterministic fallback only). Missing skip chronology at a cut boundary makes readiness uncertain and blocks qualification. Do not infer chronology from shuffled display order. See the foundation verification contract.

An encounter has an answer submission or skip; untouched generated facts do not count. First-try success requires exactly one correct submission and no skips. Corrections/skips remain in the denominator. Stopped flights contribute errors, encounters and coverage but no completion credit. Repeated families add exposure/accuracy, not distinct coverage. No calendar expiry; show evidence dates and make no retention claim. Unknown/incompatible historical scope cannot silently qualify a lock.

For an introductory tier with no non-identity families, substitute coverage of eligible introductory families and explain the exception. Validate all actual pool boundaries before applying this exception.

Numbered-level readiness uses explicitly selected level flights. Chosen-family readiness uses explicit family selections, including mixed selections, with only attributable encounters per explicitly selected family. A qualifying completed mixed flight can contribute completion credit to each selected family with retained evidence. Numbered practice does not award chosen-table trophies. Missed-fact review appears in reports/recommendations but does not earn or revoke scope readiness in this version.

Historical evidence remains eligible when scope and facts can be validated and the current coverage requirements are met; changing generator mix does not delete evidence. Explain missing coverage. Version derived rules without rewriting answer records. Reporting filters must not change readiness or restriction eligibility.

## 6. Statuses and achievements

| Status | Rule and appearance |
|---|---|
| Ready / Mastered | Every requirement met; green trophy |
| Working on | Some evidence but requirements unmet; amber crossed hammer and wrench |
| Needs work | At least 10 recent encounters and accuracy below 80%; muted red with red flag |
| Not tried | No attributable encounters; grey with diagonal white stripes |

Always include text. “Needs work” describes evidence, never the child. Each item bar is its least-complete requirement, capped at 100%; show the limiting requirement. Accuracy below 95% cannot be hidden by exposure. Preserve overall activity bars while clearly naming their scope; multiplication/division show separate Levels and Chosen families summaries, and multiplication separately shows School Test achievements. Do not display one unexplained composite mastery percentage.

An activity trophy means all applicable accuracy-readiness choices qualify; timed School Tests are separate. Before coding, enumerate applicable range choices and specify activity-bar aggregation without double-counting overlapping ranges. These technical acceptance details must honor the approved separate-achievement semantics.

School Test achievement: latest three attempted tests for that table each complete all 60 questions within two minutes with at least 57 first-try successes. Stopped attempts break the sequence; untouched tests are ignored. Historical untimed School worksheets do not qualify as timed achievements. Trophies show current qualification; permanent previously-earned awards are deferred. Explain that later evidence can lower readiness.

## 7. Parent focus controls

**Focus on unmastered practice** — “Keep practice choices focused on skills that are not yet ready. A parent can allow review.”

Default off, separate per learner; Guest starts off and never inherits another setting. Ready choices retain trophies. With focus enabled, show readable grey/striped or slashed choices and “Mastered — parent focus setting.” Provide an accessible explanation outside an unfocusable disabled control; no tooltip/color-only meaning. Partially ready activities stay open; disable only qualified child choices. Mixed selections identify restricted choices and require removal or explicit allowance, never silent edits.

Enforce at selection and immediately before starting, including voice-ready delay, Quick, remembered settings, Practice Again, Next Level, Next Table(s)/Divisor(s), School Test and alternate routes. Do not interrupt an active flight. **Allow this flight for review** grants a one-flight exception. Missed-fact review stays available and explains why. All-ready cases offer parent-controlled review or available next challenges. School Test restrictions use its timed achievement. No PIN gate initially; this is a preference, not tamper-proof control.
