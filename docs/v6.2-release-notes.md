# Fact Flight v6.2.0 — draft release notes

**Status: unreleased.** Prepared 2026-11-15 from `v6.2-development` at `affac47`, compared with main / v6.1.0 `f760615`. These notes do not authorize publication. No release date or tag has been assigned.

## Easier starts and repeat practice

Mobile setup keeps Back and Continue/Start within reach in a bottom action area. Results keeps Practice Again and eligible Next Level/Table actions nearby. The area adapts to keyboards, zoom, short screens and larger text.

## Review missed facts

From Progress, review All activities together or choose one activity, using the latest five eligible flights. From Results, review just that session. Choose up to five missed or skipped facts for a short untimed flight.

Rolling review removes a fact after first-try success in two distinct later flights following its latest mistake or skip. Repeat review refreshes eligibility rather than retaining cleared facts. Leaving the recent-history window is not a mastery claim. Untouched questions and unrecognized speech do not create missed facts.

Review defaults to One at a time on phones and Worksheet on desktop. Input and view choices are remembered per learner and separately for phone/desktop layouts. Guest results and preferences remain temporary.

## Understand table progress

Times tables at a glance shows first-try accuracy, encounters, wrong attempts, skips, distinct facts and average active time. Choose recent five eligible multiplication flights or all flights, and filter by table. Mixed reviews and original School Test contribute their relevant facts.

A fact such as 4 × 7 contributes to both tables, but only once to the overall count. Small samples are labeled. **Active time is not recall speed:** it includes input, retries and microphone preparation; compare similar practice settings and input methods.

Progress cards initially show three entries with Show all / Show fewer. Report panels and expansion controls have consistent styling. Ten-question worksheets use two columns of five on wide screens, with a single column on narrow screens. The original 60-question School Test retains its three-column worksheet and two-minute preset.

## iPhone voice

The local recovery candidate restores continuous iOS recognition across ready and answers. It removes the per-answer “Your turn” cue, natural-end scoring and per-answer cooldown. Unexpected endings require deliberate reconnection; no iOS automatic restart loop remains. Desktop retains its existing continuous behavior.

Try microphone again stays prominent. Tap or type instead returns to the same pending setup. No-result recovery stops recognition after eight seconds during readiness or twenty seconds during a running flight; missing start confirmation also times out after eight seconds. Silence alone does not identify a browser fault. Answers and the existing timer continue to be preserved. Setup-preserving refresh is offered after failed retry, with existing Guest/unsaved-data safeguards and no automatic capture after reload.

Exact iOS substitutions retain “sex” as six, “thor”/“poor” as four and “freddy” as ready only in setup. Earlier Mode H voice recognition acceptance is historical: its persistent microphone indicator failed privacy acceptance. The replacement is not yet physically accepted.

## Parent microphone control

Parent corner → Disable microphone applies to every learner and Guest in this browser, including remembered starts, missed review and reconnects. Voice choices are disabled and labeled Parent restricted. Open tabs and the diagnostic lab honor the same setting. Typed/touch practice remains available. This setting survives reloads but is not PIN-protected and cannot restrict other browsers or replace device permissions.

Completion, cancellation, confirmed navigation away and backgrounding invalidate pending work and explicitly abort an active recognizer. Ineffective Audio Session category overrides and synthesis cleanup are removed from the quiz. A bounded shutdown gate prevents overlapping manual retries; it cannot prove hardware release. Physical indicator release, background/lock behavior and follow-up voice recovery remain mandatory release gates.

## Data and compatibility

Existing history is read without rewriting it. New mixed-review sessions use session schema 3 and require a v6.2-compatible reader; the learner backup envelope remains version 1. Keep learner JSON backups before release or rollback. v6.1/v6.0 must not be treated as safe readers for new review records. Any rollback requires separate owner approval and a data-preserving plan.

Results remain local unless deliberately exported. Recognized transcripts may be stored with results and included in exports; the app does not store microphone audio. Browser recognition may use an online processing service. Temporary downloadable microphone diagnostics contain lifecycle events, not transcripts or learner details.
