## A. Menus, scrolling and input

- [ ] On Device Practice, open Math → Operations & Facts. All eight activities appear directly, in this order: Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division, Number bonds, Doubles, Halves, Squares. There is no extra Add & Subtract group click.
- [ ] Quick Practice opens the same eight activities directly. After a flight, its remembered Start again shortcut works. Back returns to the expected menu and selected choice without jumping to an unrelated activity.
- [ ] Start a five-question untimed flight in Focus, then a ten-question flight in Worksheet. On Windows, use keyboard Enter and visible controls; on iPhone, use touch/keypad. Answers, Skip, Undo, Finish and navigation remain reachable while scrolling.
- [ ] Future subject/group entries remain marked Coming Soon and do not open unfinished activities.

## B. Scoring and session modes

- [ ] In a five-question flight, make one wrong submission, correct it, and answer the other four correctly. Results should show 4/5 first try, 5/5 final correct and one wrong submission. A corrected error must not become a first-try success.
- [ ] In a separate flight, try Skip and Undo, then Finish before all generated questions are attempted. Check skips, untouched questions and stopped/completed status separately in Results and Progress.
- [ ] Try Quick, Free, Timed Practice and Custom Test. Free continues until Finish; timed choices stop at their limit. A late answer after Finish/expiry must not alter the saved result.
- [ ] Choose an optional first-try goal; an invalid goal is rejected. Returning Home during a flight asks whether to continue or finish; it must not silently discard the flight.

## C. Numbered-level content

- [ ] Sample every activity and Levels 1–5 across the session. Check readable equations, correct expected answers and advertised ranges. Include zero/identity examples where eligible, bonds with a missing part, doubles, halves and squares.
- [ ] Try several five-question flights at Squares Level 3. Bases 11 and 12 should be prominent; repeated families are expected because only two new bases exist. Do not require a unique fact for every question.
- [ ] Sample Division and Multiplication Level 3 and Addition/Subtraction Level 3. Look for an appropriate mix and any conspicuous runs of trivial or identical facts; record examples if unexpected. A five-question sample is not proof of the overall percentage targets.
- [ ] L4/L5 must not acquire easy review or identity questions merely to satisfy a quota. Batch distributions, exact pools and bounded carryover are covered by the automated audit; this device check is a practical sanity check, not a learning-effect study.

## D. Tables and division families

- [ ] Choose multiplication tables 11 and 12, first through 10 and then through 12. Confirm the displayed other-factor range, actual questions, Results heading and replay settings agree.
- [ ] Choose division by 4 and 12 with answer range through 12. Answers must be whole numbers in the chosen range; changing to through 10 must not silently keep the through-12 meaning.
- [ ] Mix two or more choices. Empty selection prevents continuing. Switching By Level / Choose Tables or Divisors preserves understandable settings.
- [ ] Check Next Table(s)/Divisor(s), including reaching 12 and no option beyond 12. Reload and inspect saved selected scopes. Backups containing these new scopes require v6.3; the old production reader is not expected to import them.

## E. Progress and readiness

- [ ] On Demo Focus, open Progress. Sessions, First-try facts and Practice time stay side by side on the phone. Mastery and Results start collapsed.
- [ ] Expand Mastery → Squares → Level 3 → What to do next. It is mastered from 29/30 first try, completed flights and coverage. Detailed evidence remains available under How readiness is calculated. This is current readiness, not proof of long-term retention.
- [ ] Compare multiplication table 7 through 10 versus through 12. Only through 10 qualifies in this fixture. School Test table 7 has a separate timed achievement.
- [ ] Expand an activity on desktop: it occupies the full available width. Close it: equal collapsed cards return. On mobile, all text remains readable without page-wide horizontal scrolling.
- [ ] Try Results activity, length, input and completion filters, and flight-log sorting. Summary cards follow Results filters; Mastery does not change because of report filters.
- [ ] Open a level report, comparable setup, facts to focus on and a saved flight. Distinguish first-try / encountered, first-try / generated, wrong submissions and unresolved wrong questions. Active time is not labelled recall speed.
- [ ] Multiplication content defaults to selectable tables 1–12; higher factors encountered in numbered practice have a separate collapsed report, not a selectable-table mastery badge. Table totals overlap and are not added to the overall total.
- [ ] Use bottom Collapse buttons at multiple depths. Each closes its own section, returns keyboard focus and scrolls to the corresponding summary.

## F. Trophies and parent focus

- [ ] On Demo Focus with focus off, follow normal Math submenus to Squares. Level 3 displays a trophy and remains selectable. Multiplication table 7 through 10 and School Test table 7 also show their appropriate trophies.
- [ ] Parent corner names the learner. Enable Focus on unmastered practice. Return to Squares: mastered Level 3 keeps its trophy, grey stripes and a readable explanation; unmastered levels remain available. A partially mastered activity must stay open.
- [ ] Select multiplication tables 7 + 8 through 12, then change to through 10. The app preserves both choices and names the restricted table 7. Try explicit Remove mastered choices; only 7 is removed. Repeat and choose Allow this flight for review instead.
- [ ] Start the allowed flight once, finish, then use Practice Again. The exception must not silently carry into another mastered flight. Check a remembered Quick shortcut and Next Level/Table route where the target is mastered.
- [ ] Change the family range: a through-10 trophy must not automatically qualify through 12. Check division by 4 through 12 independently.
- [ ] Switch to Device Practice and Guest: neither inherits Demo Focus's setting. Demo Focus retains its toggle after reload. Guest's temporary toggle clears after reload or Change learner.
- [ ] On Demo All Ready with focus on, activities are restricted but still offer a parent review route. Allow one scope and start one flight; there must be no dead end or silent substitution.
- [ ] Read the setting's explanation: it is not PIN-protected parental authentication. Missed-fact review remains available. Do not treat the preference as a tamper-proof lock.

## G. Review, repeats and next choices

- [ ] On Device Practice, create a wrong or skipped fact, then open Review missed facts from Results. Only that flight's eligible facts appear. From Progress, choose a scope and check recent eligible facts; select up to five.
- [ ] With focus on, missed-fact review still starts and explains the exception. Review retains the correct operation and scope for each mixed fact.
- [ ] Practice Again retains settings but creates a fresh flight. Next Level advances one level; at Level 5 it is absent. Next Table/Divisor retains range and input preferences.
- [ ] Inspect an older saved flight after reload. Replay restores available recorded settings and asks about anything not saved; it must not substitute an unrelated learner's setup.

## H. Original School Test

- [ ] Use the original School Test shortcut, including after chosen-table/division practice. Confirm tables 2–10, 60 questions, two minutes and the original three-column worksheet.
- [ ] Test keyboard/keypad and voice, completion and timeout. First-try accuracy, corrections, skips and saved results behave consistently.
- [ ] On Demo Focus, focus may block starting mastered School table 7. One-flight allowance permits it without altering question generation, timing, layout or scoring.

## I. Real voice and fallback

Perform these with the actual device microphone. Automated mocked recognition does not pass this group.

- [ ] Choose voice deliberately; handle the real browser permission prompt. The timer/questions wait for “ready.” Say normal answers in a five-question flight. Repeat at least three short flights and check each produces one distinct saved result.
- [ ] Include a wrong answer followed by correction, a skip, and a longer or timed flight. A final recognition callback must not double-submit an already handled interim answer or count “ready” as an answer.
- [ ] Cancel microphone setup before starting. No flight is saved. Try unavailable/denied permission and Tap or type fallback; the chosen activity, range and review facts remain intact.
- [ ] Try Reconnect when recognition stops. Background the browser, return, lock/unlock the phone and finish a flight. Observe actual microphone indicators after cancellation and completion, including after backgrounding. Record delays or failure to release.
- [ ] Retry reliability was a known v6.2 limitation. Record successful retries / attempts and exact browser behavior; do not mark this fixed merely because answer recognition works.
- [ ] Parent corner → Disable microphone remains separate from practice focus, applies as described, and blocks voice while keyboard/keypad remain available. Re-enable deliberately after this test.
- [ ] If practical, use a second candidate tab to enable practice focus while the first waits for “ready” on a mastered choice. Saying “ready” must return to the focus explanation without starting. Changing practice focus during an already running flight must not interrupt it. Close the second tab afterward; observe the save-protection rules in K.

## J. Persistence, import and learner separation

- [ ] Import both synthetic backups. Check the expected learner names/counts. Import the same file again: duplicates are skipped, not duplicated. Existing other learners remain untouched.
- [ ] Export JSON from an activity-filtered Progress view. It must include all that learner's flights; filtered CSV remains an analysis export, not an importable backup. Restore JSON in a clean candidate test context and compare scopes, totals and timestamps.
- [ ] Optionally import a copy of a fresh production backup into the isolated candidate. Verify older history remains readable, original files stay unchanged and new sessions remain separate. Never use the old production reader to test backups containing v6.3-only scopes.
- [ ] Focus preferences are not in learner backups. A new browser/context starts focus off; importing into an existing profile does not overwrite that browser's preference.
- [ ] Switch learners, rename one, reload and inspect logs. IDs/history stay with the correct learner. Guest saves/exports nothing and clears on reload/change. Delete only a disposable learner after exporting it; other learners remain intact.

## K. Two-tab save protection

- [ ] Open a second tab at the same candidate origin. It explains that another tab owns learner saving; it must not overwrite the first tab's learner data.
- [ ] Check saved results remain viewable/exportable. If an unsaved flight exists, export it before closing/reloading. Close the saving tab, reload the second, then verify a new disposable flight saves.
- [ ] Navigate away/back and reload as prompted. Never infer “saved” from a completed flight alone; check the save status and the reloaded history.

## L. Accessibility and orientation

- [ ] Windows: navigate menus/disclosures/toggle using Tab, Enter and Space. Focus remains visible; disabled choices have accessible explanations and an operable review alternative. Try browser zoom.
- [ ] iPhone: test portrait and landscape, long pages, keyboard opening/closing, bottom action bars, input focus, touch targets and returning from Results. No action should be obscured or require horizontal page scrolling.
- [ ] Check light and dark themes, long nicknames and larger text. If available, use VoiceOver/a screen reader to check disclosure names, trophies with text, restriction explanations and the toggle's state. Mark assistive-technology checks Not tested if unavailable.
- [ ] Android/tablet checks remain Not tested unless actually performed. Do not mark them passed from desktop emulation.
