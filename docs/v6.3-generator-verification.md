# v6.3 local generator increment

Owner-authorized local work, 2026-11-18, on `v6.3-development`. Not committed, pushed, deployed or released. No storage schema changes. This record supersedes the earlier planning-only status for this increment, not for the remaining v6.3 work.

## Implemented behavior

The runtime policy is `numbered-mix-v1`. Shared content definitions provide mathematical eligibility, narrow identity flags and the allocation. The allocator uses mutually exclusive buckets; identities are removed before ordinary facts are divided by earliest eligible level. Pools and answers are unchanged.

| Scope | Selected ordinary | L1 ordinary review | L2 ordinary review | Identity ceiling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| L2, except bonds | 15 | 4 | 0 | 1 |
| L3, except bonds/squares | 15 | 1 | 3 | 1 |
| Squares L3 | 12 | 2 | 5 | 1 |
| Bonds L2/L3 | 19 | 0 | 0 | 1 |

Counts are per twenty generated questions. Eligible identity capacity is used; missing identity/review capacity returns to selected-tier ordinary facts. No identities or review are imported from outside a selected pool. L1/L4/L5, chosen tables, missed-fact review and original School Test keep their existing generators.

An exact twenty-slot weighted cycle is smoothed and randomly rotated. Each bucket balances families in rounds and balances displayed variants. Selection prefers avoiding the immediately previous family, then the last three families, then a third identical answer. Allocation, validity and bucket rounds take priority when spacing conflicts with a tiny pool. Selection uses finite scans rather than an unbounded retry loop.

Carryover belongs to a learner and activity/level in memory only. It includes remaining cycle slots, family bags and recent facts. Four five-question flights accumulate one cycle. Other learners and levels have separate state. Guest state clears on learner change; all state clears on reload. Deleting learner preferences also clears that learner's generator memory. No local/session storage keys or saved answer fields were added. The policy version is exposed by the source object, not written to history.

Allocation counts **generated** questions: stopping a finite flight leaves its already-generated questions counted. Undo, corrections and revisiting existing questions do not consume new slots. Free Practice consumes a slot only when its source generates a new question. This distinction prevents claiming that every twenty encountered/answered questions must match the generation target.

Shared catalogs for numbered scopes are bounded to the requested level. Starting L2/L3 practice does not construct the large L4/L5 catalogs.

## Measured audit and tradeoffs

The preserved v6.2 baseline and new local audit each contain 30,720 synthetic flights and 230,400 generated questions over all forty activity/level scopes. Repeated audit runs use the same deterministic seeds; these are not independent learner observations.

All changed scopes met their exact allocation in sampled fresh twenty-question flights and carried chains of four five-question flights. No immediate duplicate occurred in those changed-scope samples. Unchanged scopes retain existing limitations, including 109 exact Squares L1 boundary duplicates in 384 sampled boundaries.

| Example, twenty-question flight | Released repeated-family encounters | Local measured repeated-family encounters |
|---|---:|---:|
| Squares L3 | 8 | 10 |
| Doubles / Halves L3 | 0 | 5 |
| Doubles / Halves L2 | 9 | 10 |
| Bonds L2 | 9 | 10 |

The extra repeats are the expected cost of concentrating on small selected tiers. Recent-three-family spacing must relax for Squares L3; no claim is made that increased concentration also increases novelty. The [educator guide](what-children-practice.md) contains measured before/after figures, exact targets and sequence diagnostics. Its builder preserves the separate baseline and checks runtime/documentation agreement. No learning-effect or retention claim follows from these synthetic tests.
